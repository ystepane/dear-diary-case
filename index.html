Dear Diary Case Study

Overview

"Dear Diary" is a personal journaling website designed to help users practice daily gratitude. It allows users to add daily entries, songs, moods, weather, and more, providing a comprehensive and interactive journaling experience.

Problem we are solving

The main goal of "Dear Diary" is to offer a stable, accessible platform for daily journaling, enhancing users' ability to reflect on their day and practice gratitude consistently.

Design Process

  • Inspiration: This is my personal project: I needed a stable, portable journaling tool. I explored existing journaling apps and noted their strengths and weaknesses.
  • Concept Development: Conceptualized features that would make journaling engaging and would motivate me to do it often (e.g., mood tracking, weather integration, song addition).
  • Prototyping: I first developed a prototypes to test interactions and had my friends test it for feedback.
  • Testing: I am currently using this product (website) myself!

Features

  • Daily Entries: Users can add text entries for each day.
  • Mood Tracking: Users can select an emoji that represents the mood for the day.
  • Weather: Choose what weather was there on the day.
  • Song of the Day: Users can add a song that reflects their mood or day.
  • Monthly Summaries See your selected highlights of each month! No matter how many entries you did - one or thirty, you can look at what your month was like!

Challenges

Ensuring the website is responsive and accessible. Integrating external APIs for weather and song features. Maintaining user engagement through intuitive design and functionality.

Outcome

"Dear Diary" successfully provides a stable, engaging platform for daily journaling. Users have reported increased consistency in their gratitude practice and find the additional features (mood, weather, song) enhance their journaling experience.
